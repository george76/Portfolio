About

My approach balances creativity with clarity, ensuring every product solution is intuitive, accessible, scalable, and aligned with both user needs and business goals.

I’m experienced in simplifying intricate workflows, building reusable component libraries, and embedding accessible, human-centred design practices within product and delivery teams.


A collaborative and people-focused leader, I mentor and develop high-performing design teams, fostering a culture grounded in curiosity, shared ownership, and continuous improvement. I work closely with Product, Engineering, and Research partners to align experience vision and execution, ensuring design excellence and positive impact in fast-moving, cross-functional environments.
